# Bready or Not FAQ

> Fictional sample content for a tutorial.

## Is there parking?
There are four free 30 minute spots directly in front of the shop and a bike rack by the door. The ferry lot across the street charges $2.00 an hour.

## Can I bring my dog?
Dogs are welcome on the patio but not inside. We keep a jar of free dog biscuits called Bark-uettes at the counter.

## Do you sell gift cards?
Yes, in the shop only, in any amount from $10 to $100. They do not expire.

## Do you have wifi?
No. We have bread and conversation.

## Do you have vegan options?
The Loaf Actually, Rye Me a River, and Gluten for Punishment are vegan. None of our pastries are.

## When do things sell out?
Croissants are usually gone by 10:00 am on weekends. The second bake on Friday at 4:00 pm is the best time to find warm bread late in the day.

## Do you teach classes?
Yes. The sourdough class runs on the first Sunday of each month at 2:00 pm, after we close. It costs $65.00, has 8 seats, and everyone goes home with a piece of Gerald.

## Do you sell wholesale?
Not at the moment. Our oven is the size of a large wardrobe.
