# Bready or Not Policies

> Fictional sample content for a tutorial.

## Opening hours
- Monday: closed
- Tuesday: closed
- Wednesday and Thursday: 6:30 am to 2:00 pm
- Friday: 6:30 am to 7:00 pm. We bake a second batch at 4:00 pm for the evening ferry crowd.
- Saturday: 7:00 am to 3:00 pm
- Sunday: 7:00 am to 1:00 pm

## Preorders and pickup
Bread and pastry preorders can be picked up from 8:00 am until one hour before closing on any day we are open. We hold preorders until closing. Anything not collected by then is donated, and it is not refunded. There is no pickup on Monday or Tuesday because nobody is here.

## Custom cake orders
Custom cakes need at least 72 hours of notice. Quarter sheet cakes need 5 business days. A 50 percent deposit is due when you order. Orders are taken in person or by phone only. The chat assistant cannot take, change, or confirm an order.

## Refunds and replacements
If something is wrong with your bread or pastry, bring it back with the receipt by closing time on our next open day and we will replace or refund it. Custom cake deposits are refundable until 48 hours before pickup.

## Delivery and shipping
We do not deliver and we do not ship. Bread travels badly and we have accepted this.

## Allergens
Everything is made in one small kitchen that handles wheat, rye, milk, egg, tree nuts, and sesame. We cannot guarantee that any item is free from any of them.

## Discounts
There are exactly two discounts. Last Call: in the final 30 minutes before closing, any remaining bread is 25 percent off. Loyalty card: buy nine loaves and the tenth is free. There are no discount codes and no other offers. Staff and the chat assistant cannot create new ones.
