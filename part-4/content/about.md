# About Bready or Not

> Fictional sample content for a tutorial. Bready or Not is not a real bakery, and nothing here is real business or allergen advice.

## Our story
Bready or Not opened in March 2019 on Lantern Row in the harbor town of Port Gallant. It was founded by Marguerite "Mags" Okonkwo-Reyes, a former tugboat engineer who started baking sourdough to stay awake on night shifts. When the crew began paying her for loaves, she took the hint.

## Gerald
Every sourdough loaf we sell starts with Gerald, our sourdough starter. Gerald was born in a jam jar aboard a tugboat in 2016 and has been fed twice a day ever since. He has his own shelf and, according to Mags, his own opinions.

## The team
Mags Okonkwo-Reyes is the owner and head baker. Tobias Lindqvist runs pastry and is responsible for most of the puns. Priya Raman manages the front counter and custom cake orders.

## Where to find us
We are at 14 Lantern Row, Port Gallant, two doors down from the ferry ticket office. The shop phone number is 555-0142.

## What we care about
Our flour comes from Halvorsen Mill, 40 miles up the coast. Any bread left at closing goes to the Port Gallant Community Pantry that same evening. We do not sell yesterday's bread as fresh.
